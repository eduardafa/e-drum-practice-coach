Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

In short:

  • You can practise without an account. Then nothing about you leaves your browser.
  • If you sign in, the app keeps only which service you used (GitHub or Google) and that service's account number for you. Never your email, name or picture.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. Nothing is sold or shared.

Kept in your browser

Without an account, all of this stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Settings: tempo, metronome, skill level, latency calibration, the exercise on the stand and similar choices.
  • Practice history: each finished session and tempo-trainer run: the exercise, tempo, when and for how long you played, your time zone, and how each note went (its timing and how hard you hit).
  • Your kit's signals: the notes your drum kit sends over MIDI are scored in the browser as you play. They are never recorded or sent, even when you're signed in.

You can delete your history with Clear history in the History panel, or delete everything by clearing this site's data in your browser.

If you sign in

Signing in lets your practice history follow you to your other devices. You sign in on GitHub's or Google's own page; the app never sees your password. In return, the app receives and stores:

WhatWhyKept
Which service you used, and its account number for you (for example, GitHub user 12345678) To recognise you next time Until you ask for your account to be deleted
A session for each signed-in browser (a scrambled form of the sign-in cookie, and when it expires) To keep you signed in Up to 30 days, renewed while you keep using the app, or until you sign out
Your practice history, as described above, once syncing is available To show it on your other devices Until you delete it or your account

The app asks GitHub for no permissions beyond your public profile, reads only your account number from it, and discards the access it was given. From Google it asks only for basic sign-in (openid): no email, no profile, no access to anything else in your account.

Cookies

Only two, both needed for signing in, and neither used for tracking:

  • __Host-oauth: set while you sign in, to check that the answer from GitHub or Google belongs to the sign-in you started. It lasts 10 minutes.
  • __Host-session: keeps you signed in. It lasts 30 days and is renewed when you use the app.

Scripts on the page can't read them, and other websites can't use them to act as you.

Where it lives

The app runs on Cloudflare. Signed-in data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database in Eastern North America. Like any website host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, under its own privacy policy. The app itself doesn't log or keep them.

When you sign in, GitHub's or Google's privacy policy applies to what happens on their pages.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out in the History panel to end that browser's session.
  • Delete your account: a button for this is coming. Until then, ask by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page (don't post anything personal there) and your account and everything stored with it will be deleted.
  • You can also remove the app's access from your GitHub settings or Google account.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes too. The page's full history is public in the project's code.
